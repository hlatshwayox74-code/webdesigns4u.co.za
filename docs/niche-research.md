# Niche research behind the website concepts

Each "Build my website" concept is built from a playbook for the visitor's business type.
The playbooks (in `assets/js/concept-engine.js`, `PLAYBOOKS`) encode what leading websites in
that niche consistently do, plus the one thing Web Designs4U does better.

Researched October 2026. Re-check yearly.

| Niche | What the leaders do | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Real Estate | Image-led hero; listings and neighbourhood pages are core; restrained editorial type; a clear contact prompt; fresh recurring content | [Luxury Presence](https://www.luxurypresence.com/blogs/best-realtor-websites/), [The Close](https://theclose.com/real-estate-website-templates/) |
| Restaurant | Hero that makes you hungry; native web menu (not a PDF) with prices and dietary notes; booking button always visible; hours, parking, map; live reviews | [Framer](https://framer.com/blog/restaurant-website-design-examples/), [Dorik](https://dorik.com/blog/restaurant-website-examples) |
| Construction | Full-width imagery of real projects; portfolio by sector; quote CTA throughout; certifications, safety record, awards; case-study outcomes | [Azuro Digital](https://azurodigital.com/construction-website-examples/) |
| Driving School | Outcome headline; upfront packages and prices; online booking with instant confirmation; instructor profiles; pass results and Google reviews near the top; local area pages | [InsideA](https://insidea.com/blog/marketing/driving-school/website-design-ideas-for-driving-schools), [Webflow](https://webflow.com/list/driving-school) |
| Clothing | Editorial photography that tells a style story; products shown worn and in motion; restrained typography; fit and sizing guidance; transparent pricing | [KrishaWeb](https://www.krishaweb.com/blog/best-fashion-website-designs/) |
| Ecommerce | Bold product or lifestyle hero; clean product grid; multiple views and specs; ratings on product pages; clear shipping and returns; streamlined checkout; guided product quizzes | [Liquid Web](https://www.liquidweb.com/blog/ecommerce-website-design/) |
| Beauty | Tap-to-book online booking; work gallery by service; team introductions; long-term client reviews and Google reviews; simple five-page navigation; product shop | [Adalo](https://www.adalo.com/posts/seven-essential-features-for-salon-and-spa-websites-in-2024---and-the-platforms-to-build-them/), [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/blog/beauty-website-examples) |
| Fitness | Video/imagery of real classes; live class schedule with one-click booking; membership prices shown (hiding them is a known mistake); coach profiles; transformations | [WOD Guru](https://wod.guru/blog/gym-website-design/) |
| Professional Services | Strong value statement; plain-language practice/service pages; people profiles with direct access; concrete results; click-to-call consultation CTA; credentials | [Azuro Digital](https://azurodigital.com/law-firm-website-examples/), [CyberOptik](https://cyberoptik.net/blog/20-best-accounting-firm-websites) |
| Hospitality | Photography of the real experience; booking engine visible on every page; every room with its own identity; local experiences and dining; direct-booking benefits | [KrishaWeb](https://www.krishaweb.com/blog/best-hotel-website-designs/) |
| Automotive | Promise headline with real shop photos; book-a-service and phone above the fold; scannable services; warranties, certifications, years in business; third-party reviews; free estimates | [Freshy](https://freshysites.com/blog/top-auto-repair-websites/) |
| Education | Lead with proof (results); subjects reachable from the homepage; open pricing; tutor profiles; low-commitment trial lesson; specific named testimonials | [KrishaWeb](https://www.krishaweb.com/blog/best-tutoring-website-designs/) |
| Other / small business | Specific hero image and plain headline; one main next step; value cards under the hero; reviews and trust badges; tap-to-call; hours and area | [UENI](https://ueni.com/blog/18-small-business-website-examples-you-can-learn-from/) |
