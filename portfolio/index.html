Case 01 · Web Designs4U

A website that sells websites.

StrategyStory designHTML · CSS · JSLocal SEOLead capture

A 12-chapter scroll story that walks a business owner from "my customers are online" to "this is what my website could look like", then hands them straight to WhatsApp.

webdesigns4u.co.za/#ch-transform

The brief

Most small businesses in Gqeberha have never seen what a good website does for them, so a list of services wouldn't land. The site had to show the difference before it asked for anything.

The idea

Treat the homepage like a short film. Each chapter makes one point, and the visitor's own business name shows up in the design by chapter seven.

What I built

  • 12 chaptersA full-screen scroll story with chapter navigation, optional sound, and a live clock that reads Gqeberha time.
  • Before / afterA drag slider that turns a 2005-style website into a modern one, on desktop or mobile view.
  • Instant previewVisitors pick their industry, goal and style, and a matching website template is generated for them on the spot.
  • 8-step briefA guided "Build my website" form. If sending fails, their answers go to WhatsApp already typed, so no lead is lost.
  • Found locallyPage titles, descriptions and share images written for "website design Gqeberha / Port Elizabeth".

Case 02 · Nexcharge

One product. One page. No confusion.

Shopify custom themeProduct copyBundles & offersProduct video

A store for a single magnetic powerbank, designed so a stranger understands the product, picks a deal and checks out without leaving the page.

nexcharge.co.za

The brief

A new store with no reviews yet, selling a gadget people usually want to hold before they buy. It needed to feel like a real brand, answer every doubt, and make the bigger order the obvious choice.

The idea

Sell the moment, not the battery. The page follows a phone through one day, from the commute at 07:10 to a night out at 21:00, and the powerbank is always attached.

What I built

  • Deal pickerThree offers side by side (single, with 240W charger, buy 3 get the charger free) with the saving worked out for the buyer.
  • A day in a lifeA time-stamped story section with real product photos at each moment of the day.
  • Honest specsPlain copy about what 10000mAh means for your phone, instead of a top-up number that won't match.
  • Watch it workA 20-second product video section, a 7-photo gallery and an 8-question FAQ.
  • Checkout readyShopify checkout, Yoco payments, tracked delivery info and a buy bar that follows you down the page.

Case 03 · Elyven

Eyewear built for South African light.

Brand identityShopify custom themeInteractive lens demoProduct finder

A new local eyewear label that needed to look established from day one, and to explain lenses in a way the big brands don't bother to.

elyven.co.za

The brief

Fashion eyewear is crowded with logos. Elyven needed a reason to exist and a look that could sit next to premium labels without copying any of them.

The idea

Start where the light does. South Africa has some of the highest UV levels in the world, so the brand leads with the lens and explains every one in plain words.

What I built

  • IdentityName treatment, editorial serif typography and a photo direction built around coastal roads and hard sun.
  • Lens simulatorTap Polarised, UV400, Photochromic or Blue-light and see what each lens changes on the same coastal photo.
  • Pair finderTwo questions (where you'll wear them, your budget) and the store shows the frames that fit.
  • Shop by useCollections organised by sun, screen and everyday instead of by logo, with reviews and options on every card.
  • Offers built inBuy 2 save 10% and free delivery over R1 000, applied automatically at checkout and announced in the top bar.

Case 04 · Vessara

Hair care, sold as a routine.

Shopify custom themeBrand lookRoutine builderLaunch ads

A South African hair care store with 21 products. Instead of a wall of bottles, the whole shop is arranged as four steps: Prepare, Style, Finish, Maintain.

vessara.co.za · new theme

The brief

The store was live on Shopify's default theme. It worked, but it looked like every other new store, and nothing told a shopper which products belong together.

The idea

People don't buy hair care one bottle at a time, they follow a routine. So the store teaches the routine first and lets the shopper buy the whole thing in one tap.

What I built

  • Four-step shopEvery product lives in one step, each with its own chapter, photo and short guide to what it does.
  • Routine builderPick one product per step, see the running total, then add the whole routine to the bag or go straight to checkout.
  • Brand lookSunset photography, an elegant serif with a clean sans, and a sand, mocha and gold palette that feels premium.
  • The journalA guide for each step of the routine, so the store has a reason for people to come back.
  • Ads to matchLaunch ads for the rosemary scalp oil and the heat protection spray, shown in the ads section below.
